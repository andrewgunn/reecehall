    How a job runs

    From first visit to handover.

    Every build follows the same order. You'll always know which stage we're at, what comes next and what it costs.

    1. 01

      Visit & quote

      Reece visits, listens to what you want and gives you a clear, itemised price. Free, with no obligation.

    2. 02

      Plans & permissions

      We work with your architect, or ours, and guide you through planning and building control.

    3. 03

      Structure

      Foundations, steels and walls. Knocking through and opening up, done safely and signed off.

    4. 04

      Roof & shell

      Cut roofs, dormers, lanterns and slate. The house is weathertight before the inside starts.

    5. 05

      Services & first fix

      Underfloor heating, electrics, plumbing and insulation, all coordinated around the build.

    6. 06

      Finish & handover

      Kitchens, bathrooms, floors and joinery. Then we walk round the whole job with you before we hand over.

    Brand refresh & domain.

    The logo stays exactly as it is. People already know it from the vans and site boards. What's new is everything around it: a website and colours based on the things you actually build with, like gritstone, slate and lime mortar, with the logo orange as the one bright colour.

    What stays, what's new
    • Logo unchanged. Same house-and-H mark, same orange, same lettering. It's now drawn as a sharp vector file, so it prints cleanly at any size, from a phone icon to a van door.
    • Orange is the one bright colour. Keeping everything else calm makes the logo stand out.
    • Slate and stone around it. A soft blue-black and warm stone tones that sit well with photos of your builds.
    • Wide, heavy headings. Used on the website only. They read like site hoarding and signwriting.
