    How a job runs

    From first visit to handover.

    Every build follows the same order. You'll always know which stage we're at, what comes next and what it costs.

    1. 01

      Visit & quote

      Reece visits, listens to what you want and gives you a clear, itemised price. Free, with no obligation.

    2. 02

      Plans & permissions

      We work with your architect, or ours, and guide you through planning and building control.

    3. 03

      Structure

      Foundations, steels and walls. Knocking through and opening up, done safely and signed off.

    4. 04

      Roof & shell

      Cut roofs, dormers, lanterns and slate. The house is weathertight before the inside starts.

    5. 05

      Services & first fix

      Underfloor heating, electrics, plumbing and insulation, all coordinated around the build.

    6. 06

      Finish & handover

      Kitchens, bathrooms, floors and joinery. Then we walk round the whole job with you before we hand over.

    Brand refresh & domain.

    The current orange-and-black house logo is recognisable, so this keeps the orange and the house-and-H idea and makes it simpler and stronger. The new look is based on the things you actually build with: gritstone, slate and lime mortar.

    Logo
    R Hall & SonDevelopments · Sheffield
    What changed
    • A simpler mark. A roof pitch over an H. It reads at van-sticker size, on a hi-vis back and as a tiny phone icon.
    • Orange stays. People already know it from the vans and boards, so it's now the only accent colour.
    • Black becomes slate. A softer blue-black that sits better next to stone photography.
    • Wide, heavy lettering. It reads like site hoarding and signwriting, not like a tech company.

    Domain options
    rhallandsondevelopments.co.uk

    The current address is 24 characters before the .co.uk, and people can read “rhallandson” as “rhalland son”. It's hard to say over the phone and easy to mistype.

    • hallandson.co.ukTop pick
      Short, says the name, and UK customers trust .co.uk.
    • rhallandson.co.uk
      Keeps the R to match the company name.
    • hallandsonbuild.co.uk
      Says what you do. Good on a van.
    • hallandsonhomes.co.uk
      Suits a new-build focus.
    • reecehall.co.uk
      Your own name. Useful for a personal email address.
    • hallandson.uk
      Shortest option. Worth buying next to the .co.uk.
    • rhallandson.com
      A .com backup, so nobody else can take it.
    • hallandson.build
      Modern ending, but less familiar to customers.
