    How a job runs

    From first visit to handover.

    Every build follows the same order. You'll always know which stage we're at, what comes next and what it costs.

    1. 01

      Visit & quote

      Reece visits, listens to what you want and gives you a clear, itemised price. Free, with no obligation.

    2. 02

      Plans & permissions

      We work with your architect, or ours, and guide you through planning and building control.

    3. 03

      Structure

      Foundations, steels and walls. Knocking through and opening up, done safely and signed off.

    4. 04

      Roof & shell

      Cut roofs, dormers, lanterns and slate. The house is weathertight before the inside starts.

    5. 05

      Services & first fix

      Underfloor heating, electrics, plumbing and insulation, all coordinated around the build.

    6. 06

      Finish & handover

      Kitchens, bathrooms, floors and joinery. Then we walk round the whole job with you before we hand over.
